# Stavya SSIE — Patient-Centric Spine Care Clinical Platform

A comprehensive Clinical Patient Care platform for **Stavya Spine Hospital & Research Institute**, architected with the **Spine Patient at the exact center** of every clinical decision, department, and workflow.

## 🚀 Live Deployment
- Built with React 18, Vite 6, Tailwind CSS v4, Lucide Icons, and TypeScript.
- Pre-configured for one-click deployment on **Vercel** (`vercel.json`), **Netlify** (`netlify.toml`), or **GitHub Pages**.

### One-Click Deploy Build Settings
- **Framework Preset**: Vite
- **Build Command**: `npm run build`
- **Output Directory**: `dist`
- **Install Command**: `npm install`

## 👥 Integrated Staff & Services
- 213 confirmed Stavya Hospital staff members integrated across all clinical services.
- 13 clinical services: Spine Surgery, Anesthesia & PAC, Operating Theatres (OT 1–6), Spine Radiology & PACS, CSSD, IPD & HDU Nursing, Physio & Spine Rehab, Clinical Pharmacy, Clinical Coordination, Medical Officers (24×7), Patient Experience & PROs, Quality & Research, Ward & Bed Matrix.
- Dual operating modes: Clinician Command Mode & Patient & Family Companion Portal Mode.
